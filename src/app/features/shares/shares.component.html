@if (showInfoModal) {

How shares are calculated

Share Percentage

Each member's ownership percentage is based on their contributions to the selected contribution types divided by total contributions.

Member Share % = (Member's Contributions) ÷ (Total Contributions) × 100

Estimated Value

The estimated value applies that share percentage to the total pool value, which includes contributions, fines, loan interest, investment returns, and penalties.

Member Value = Share % × Total Pool Value
Merry-Go-Round and Welfare contributions are excluded from calculations since they're distributed back to members.
